{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The enrolment form, DEV.md S3.1 and FRONTEND.md F7. One template for all three places it appears: its own page, the contact page and every course page. Everything is a cotton primitive — tech.md section 7 forbids a page writing its own field markup, and a form is exactly where that temptation is strongest. The six states F7 names, and where each one lives: 1 rest the fields as they render here 2 focus the base layer, A.5: a second pixel of ink from the inside, so nothing shifts 3 field error c-field and c-checkbox, both wiring aria-describedby to the message and aria-invalid to the control 4 sending the two button labels below, swapped by .htmx-request, with hx-disabled-elt taking the button out of reach 5 success _form_result.html replaces the whole form 6 server down the region below, revealed by app.js when htmx reports a failed response — the form itself is never swapped, so everything the visitor typed is still in it Without javascript the same POST goes through normally and lands on the thank you page, so none of the above is load bearing. hx-target is "this" rather than the id: the form replaces itself, so nothing depends on there being exactly one of them on the page. form_id exists for the kitchen sink, which shows several at once and would otherwise repeat an id. Takes: form the bound or unbound LeadForm. form_id optional, defaults to lead-form. {% endcomment %}
