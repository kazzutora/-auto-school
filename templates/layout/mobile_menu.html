A native dialog opened with showModal(), for the same reason c-modal is one: the focus lock, Esc and the focus restore are the browser's job and it does them right. A panel built out of a div and a boolean has to re-implement all three, and the shift-tab wrap is the part that always ends up wrong. Full screen, because a menu that only covers half the page leaves the reader looking at two things at once. The dialog's own margin and max-width are reset to nothing; without that the ua stylesheet centres it in a box. Kontakt is a first level item here exactly as it is on the desktop. Hiding it in a submenu was the main defect of the old site, tech.md section 7, and a mobile panel is the easiest place to lose it again.
