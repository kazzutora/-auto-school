{% comment %} Carousel, REDESIGN.md B.5. Props: id, per_view, and the slot. No carousel library, and C.2 is what forbids it: the scrolling, the snapping, the momentum and the swipe are all the browser's own, and a library would be 20-plus KB to reimplement them worse. What is left for javascript is two buttons, and static/js/motion.js answers them with scrollBy — about twenty lines, shared with every other rail on the site. Keyboard: the track is a focusable scroll container, so Left and Right scroll it natively once it has focus, and Tab walks the cards in order because they are in the document in order. That is the whole keyboard story and none of it is written here. tabindex="0" on the track is what a scrollable region needs to be reachable at all — without it a keyboard user can reach the cards but never the scrollbar, and a screen reader has no way to announce the region. The arrows are hidden below md. A phone swipes; a row of 44px targets over the content is what you get instead of the content. per_view is the count at lg. Below that the basis steps down on its own rather than reading the prop, because tailwind needs whole class names at build time and a basis- glued together at render is a class it never sees. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %}
