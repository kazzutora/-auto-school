That is not only shorter: the first version of the home page handed the browser the bare JPEG for the two bands, which is the fallback nobody modern receives, and spent 180 KB of the 500 KB first-screen budget in C.6 on the difference. A typo in one of nine paths does the same thing silently. `crop_mobile` is the hero's case: a 16:9 hero on a portrait phone is a letterbox with the subject cropped out of it, so the phone gets a 4:5 cut of its own. Both live in ONE with a media query on the mobile sources — two pictures hidden from each other by a css class would download both files, which is the opposite of the point. `priority` marks the LCP element: fetchpriority high, eager, and no lazy. Every other photograph on the site is lazy, C.6. `decorative` is for a photograph used as a background with text over it.
