{% comment %} Course card, tech.md section 7. The category tile, reused by /kursy/ rather than growing a second one of its own. The category code carries the tile in the h1 scale — B, B+E, C+E read as signage. Under it the two facts a visitor actually compares: the age they have to be, and what it costs. A course with no price says so; a blank where a number belongs reads as an error. The whole tile is the link, and the ring lands on the tile. It draws no frame of its own any more. It used to carry its own border, its own background and its own 4px lift, which was three ways of saying "card" beside a c-card that already says it — and the lift disagreed with B.5, which puts the move at 2px and lets the shadow do the rest. u-card and u-card-link now carry all of it, so the tile cannot drift from every other card on the site. {% endcomment %} {% load core_ui i18n %} {% if course.code %}
