On the left the crumbs, the handwritten eyebrow, the h1 and one line of lead, with room under them for whatever the page adds (the phone card on Zapisy). On the right the `visual` slot, which runs past the container to the edge of the screen from lg — a car cut off by the container's gutter reads as a mistake, and the page's own layout decides what goes there. Short on purpose. The band that came before it put 160px of nothing between the menu and the heading and left the right half empty; this one starts 32px under the menu and ends where its taller half does. `bleed` is on by default; a visual that is text, the steps on Zapisy, stays inside the container, where a line of it can be read to its end. `photo` turns the band into a photograph, the course page's: a static path to a picture whose left part is clear of anything that matters. The sharp copy sits on the right and fades out to the left; under the words the same picture is blurred and dimmed, so the left half keeps the photograph's colour without any of its detail competing with the heading. u-photo-ground sets the white pair, and scripts/check_photo_contrast.py measures it like the home hero. On a phone the photograph goes under the words rather than behind them.
