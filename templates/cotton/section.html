{% comment %} A section, ROSE.md B.8 and C.1. Grounds alternate blush, pale, blush — and never white, B.8. `wine` is the dark band, and there is one of those on a page: the closing invitation, or the enquiry block on the home page. A second turns the rhythm into stripes. `dark` is kept as the old name for `wine`, and `brand` no longer is one: under the v25 palette `brand` meant the near-black card, and under ROSE.md it means the crimson. A name that quietly swapped grounds would have been the worst of both, so the old callers were moved and the word now means what it says. The eyebrow is the handwritten note of B.7 point 3 — `Z nami zdasz`, `O nas`, `Nasze kursy`. One per section, which is why it lives here as a slot rather than being written into each page: a component can only render one of them, so the rule holds by construction rather than by review. `edge` and `corner` place a brush stroke, B.7 point 1: only at the edges of a section and behind photographs, never under a paragraph. The section turns `relative` and clips itself when it carries one, because a stroke that bleeds past the edge is the point and a horizontal scrollbar is not. {% endcomment %}
